# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming and harassment-free community for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Respectful and constructive feedback
- Empathy toward other contributors
- Focus on project goals and user value

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation
- Personal attacks and trolling
- Publishing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and may take corrective action when needed.
